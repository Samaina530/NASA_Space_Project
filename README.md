# NASA_Space_Project
It is an interactive space museum about NASA spacecraft that fell silent on Mars and the Moon, with a rover game and I built for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

- Beyond the Signal
An interactive space museum about NASA spacecraft that stopped working
on Mars and the Moon. Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026,
for the challenge about telling the story of NASA's discarded equipment.

* What it does
- Explore 8 missions: Opportunity, Spirit, Mars Climate Orbiter,
  Mars Polar Lander, Sojourner, MAVEN, Lunar Prospector and LADEE.
- Filter by Mars or Moon and open each spacecraft's story.
- Click numbered parts on a spacecraft diagram to learn what they did.
- Watch a typewriter-style signal archive.
- Play a rover driving game on Mars or the Moon, and learn a real
  mission fact when you reach the destination.

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks.
